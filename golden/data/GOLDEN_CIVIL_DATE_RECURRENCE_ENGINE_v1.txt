PORT CONTRACT
CivilDate(year,month,day); ordered DateSpan(start,end).
RecurrenceRule: weekly, monthly(day, overflow), yearly(month,day,leapPolicy),
fixedDate, nthWeekday, lastWeekday, lastDayOfMonth, fixedRange, oneOff,
authorityDefined and lunar/opaqueExternal.
resolve(rule, interval, overrides) -> spans, resolved flag, unresolvedReason.
SeriesOccurrenceId = seriesId + civil day + exceptionRevision.
Timezone is a scheduling adapter input, not a hidden date arithmetic default.

INVARIANTS
- No silent rollover: Jan31->Feb28/29 only under explicit CLAMP policy.
- Feb29 yearly: explicit Feb28, Mar1 or SKIP; no implicit guess.
- Reject invalid month/day, nonexistent 5th weekday, invalid interval.
- Correct last weekday, nth weekday and inclusive/exclusive range semantics.
- DST/timezone change must not shift civil all-day event.
- Unresolved lunar/authority date without trusted override remains UNRESOLVED.
- Stable recurrence series identity and no duplicates on reprocessing.
- Preserve legacy nonrecurring event serialization and edited exceptions.
- Product-owned notification logic stays outside engine.
