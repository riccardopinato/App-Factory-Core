INVARIANTS

  BEFORE ranking; only expose material that requester may access.
- Identical inputs/seed/rules yield stable tie ordering (ID comparator).
- Deduplicate by source canonical ID and respect per-group/source caps.
- Explain recommendations via explicit rule reason codes, no opaque AI claim.
- Time-based events use civil recurrence rules; prior-year anniversaries only.
- Only photos explicitly linked to a memory may be surfaced in that memory.
- Empty pool yields honest empty list, not fabricated output.
- Store minimal preferences locally where possible; no implicit telemetry.
- The scoring policy for photography and the rediscovery narrative remain
  domain plugins, not universal weights.
